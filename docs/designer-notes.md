## Stephen West

- Origin story: https://www.news-gazette.com/news/local/knit-the-big-time-stephen-west/article_edeb64ee-3e13-5f76-bc8e-4bf40c132149.html
- Original blog: https://westknits.blogspost.com
- Started working at a yarn shop while studying dance at the University of Illinois
- His first designs were made in collaboration with the yarn store where he worked
- He was not a proflific blogger in his early days, 2007-2008 only have a few posts, all featuring knitting but pretty informal and little engagement.
- He starts to pick up engagement towards the end of 2009 when he publishes the daybreak shawl. https://westknits.blogspot.com/2009/08/daybreak-pattern.html
- This blog post details the response: It was made by other knitters, picked up as a featured pattern by knitpicks, etc. Around the same time, he also started getting requests for commissioned work. https://westknits.blogspot.com/2009/08/blog-posts-about-daybreak.html
- In the same months, he follows the daybreak shawl with herbivore. https://westknits.blogspot.com/2009/08/herbivore.html

## Veera Välimäki

## Andrea Mowry

Andrea Mowry has always been a bit of an enigma. This isn't the first time I have been curious about who she is and how she started, but I have never found a bio. She doesn't even say where she lives. She is constantly taking photos of herself, perfectly styled in her colorful knits, but there's not much to find underneath that image.

Here's what I can piece together from the Instagram record. In 2014, she was just a mom posting photos of her pregnancies and her kids and occasional knitting projects. She had a very typical millennial #vscocam aesthetic.

Her project photos in this era were largely flat-lay, not photos with her modeling them. Soon (by mid 2014) she started modeling her completed sweaters (not her own designs yet), and picking up some regular visitors and commenters just with her photos.

In August 2014, one of her finished projects was featured in a Ravelry thread. SSKALONG
